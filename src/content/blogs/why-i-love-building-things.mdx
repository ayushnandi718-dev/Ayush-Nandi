# Why I Love Building Things

There's a moment every developer knows: the first time your code runs.

You start with nothing.

No buttons. No pages. No users.

An empty folder.

## The Magic of Creation

Then, one commit at a time, something appears.

First a button. Then a page. Then a whole application that people can actually use.

That transformation — from nothing to something — never gets old. It's the closest thing to magic I've found that's legal to do at 3 AM with an energy drink.

## It Keeps You Going

The projects I care about most are the ones where I saw the whole journey:

- A broken script
- A working prototype
- A finished product

Building is the reason I keep going. Every project is a small act of creation — and that's worth loving.
