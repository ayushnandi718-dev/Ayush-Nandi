# Lessons from My First Freelance Client

The first client doesn't hire you because you're the best developer in the world.

They hire you because they trust you.

## What I Learned

That first project taught me more than any tutorial ever could:

- **Scope matters** — a clear brief beats a brilliant guess
- **Communication matters** — a confused client is a stressed client
- **Delivering beats perfecting** — ship something on time, improve it later

## The Reality Check

Fully remote. Bouncing between timezones. Weekend deadlines.

It was messy, it was stressful, and I loved every minute of it.

Freelancing isn't just about code — it's about being reliable enough that a stranger hands you their idea and sleeps better for it. That responsibility is the real job.
