# Motion Makes Interfaces Feel Human

Animations should answer questions, not just look cool.

Every transition should tell the user:

- **Where did it come from?**
- **Where did it go?**
- **What changed?**

## Why Motion Matters

Without motion, interfaces feel like spreadsheets.

With motion, they feel like conversations.

A button that slides toward the next step is a hand guiding you. A card that gently scales on hover is an invitation. These tiny moments turn a product into an experience.

## The Golden Rule

If the animation doesn't add meaning, it adds noise.

Subtle is memorable. Loud is forgettable. The best motion is the motion nobody notices — because everything just feels right.
