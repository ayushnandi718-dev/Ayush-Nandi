# The 2 AM Bug That Taught Me Everything

There's a unique silence at 2 AM when your application suddenly refuses to compile.

No helpful errors.

No stack trace.

Just:

```
Build Failed
```

## The Hunt

After four hours of frustration — reading the same files on loop, commenting out blocks, checking for typos that were never there — I finally found it.

The problem?

A missing closing bracket.

## The Lesson

Programming has a funny way of reminding us that computers don't care how tired we are. They follow the rules, and one missing character is a missing character.

But that night taught me more than the fix. It taught me to debug with my head, not my frustration:

- Take a breath before touching anything
- Read the code aloud, top to bottom
- Suspect the obvious thing first — it usually is

The bugs that humble you at 2 AM are the ones you never forget.
