# Building My Portfolio from Scratch

Your portfolio isn't supposed to impress everyone.

It's supposed to convince one person: the recruiter, client, or founder who's visiting your website.

## The Three Goals

When I started building mine, I had three goals:

- **Fast** — it loads before the visitor finishes reading the URL
- **Beautiful** — the first impression matters, and there's only one
- **Memorable** — they should remember my name, not just my tech stack

Everything else was secondary.

## Tech Stack

- Next.js
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Framer Motion
- GSAP
- MDX

## Lessons Learned

Animation should support the experience — not become the experience.

Every effect I added had to answer a question. If it didn't make the site faster to understand or harder to forget, it was cut. That restraint is what made the final result feel intentional.
