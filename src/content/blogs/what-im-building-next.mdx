# What I'm Building Next

Every developer has a list of projects that never made it past the "someday" folder.

Here's mine — and this time, I'm shipping it.

## The Roadmap

- **AI-powered tools** — apps that make everyday tasks smarter, not just show off AI
- **Better portfolio interactions** — this site is only the beginning
- **Experimental animations** — pushing what the browser can do
- **Open-source components** — the stuff I wish existed, free for everyone

## Why

I stopped waiting for the "right" time and the "perfect" stack.

The only way to find out if an idea works is to build it. Some of these will fail — and that's fine. The ones that don't fail are the ones worth keeping.

Stay tuned.
