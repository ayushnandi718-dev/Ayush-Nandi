# Why Every Developer Should Learn Design

Design isn't decoration — it's communication.

## The Four Pillars

A developer who understands these four things builds better products:

- **Typography** — legible, intentional, and sized with hierarchy
- **White space** — the space between elements is a design decision too
- **Hierarchy** — what's most important should look most important
- **Color psychology** — blue is trust, orange is energy, red is urgency

## Why It Matters

When I started learning design, I stopped just implementing screens — I started questioning them.

Why is this button here? Why is that text smaller? What is this color saying?

Understanding design made me a better developer because I started building products, not just pages. The best engineers speak the designer's language, and the best designers respect the engineer's constraints.
