# Designing in the Dark

Dark themes aren't just black backgrounds.

A good dark UI balances:

- **Contrast** — enough to read, not enough to strain
- **Spacing** — room for elements to breathe
- **Motion** — subtle, purposeful, never distracting
- **Shadows** — depth without murkiness
- **Accent colors** — one strong voice, not a chorus

## The Accent

Orange became my primary accent because it feels energetic without being distracting. It draws the eye to the things that matter and stays quiet everywhere else.

Every glow has a purpose. Every shadow creates depth.

## The Test

Good interfaces disappear. Great interfaces feel alive.

When you're designing in the dark, ask yourself one question at the end of the day: does this still feel good at midnight, on a cheap laptop, with the brightness at 30%? If yes, you're done.
